# TUM Master's Roadmap & Milestones

## 1. Target Profile
- **Target Degree:** M.Sc. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence[cite: 4]
- **Target University:** Technical University of Munich (TUM)[cite: 4]
- **Why TUM:** It is renowned for its strong AI focus and consistently ranks among the top in Europe[cite: 4]

## 2. Institutional Verification (Anabin)
- **Current Institution:** Amity University (CSE AI and ML)[cite: 4]
- **Recognition Status:** Confirmed "H+" status on the Anabin database[cite: 4]
- **Significance:** An "H+" status means the university is officially recognized in Germany, making the bachelor's degree equivalent to a German qualification[cite: 4]

## 3. APS Certificate Tracking
- **What it is:** A mandatory academic verification document for Indian students to prove the authenticity of educational degrees for German universities[cite: 4]
- **Verification Body:** Central verification by the German Embassy and DAAD[cite: 4]
- **Application Timeline:** Can apply in the middle of the fourth year, provided you are officially in your final year of study[cite: 4]
- **Where to Apply:** Online through the official APS India website[cite: 4]
- **Cost:** ₹18,000 (non-refundable application fee)[cite: 4]

## 4. General Requirements
- **Language:** Proof of English proficiency required (IELTS or TOEFL)[cite: 4]
- **Documentation:** Preliminary documentation must be submitted via uni-assist[cite: 4]
