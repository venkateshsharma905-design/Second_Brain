# Wie bitte?

- **Meaning:** Excuse me? / I beg your pardon? / Could you repeat that?[cite: 5]
- **Usage:** Essential survival phrase when you do not understand spoken German. Much more polite than just saying "Was?" (What?).

## Example Sentence
> **Person A:** Der Flug hat Verspätung.
> **Person B:** Wie bitte?
